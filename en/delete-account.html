Account and data deletion

You can delete your Nart - Learn Circassian account and all of your data on our servers in two ways.

In the app

  1. Open the app and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Account > Delete account.
  3. Re-authenticate and confirm the deletion.

If you cannot access the app

Send an email with the subject "Account deletion request" to support@w8digital.com from the email address linked to your account. We complete requests within 30 days.

What is deleted

Your account information (email, user ID, name), profile information and all learning data are permanently deleted. Data stored on your device is removed when you delete the app. No data is kept unless required by law.

Note: Deleting your account does not cancel a Nart Premium subscription. Cancel it in your App Store or Google Play account settings.

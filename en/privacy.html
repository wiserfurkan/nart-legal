Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 12, 2026

Nart - Learn Circassian (the "app") is provided by Wiser Digital Solutions A.Ş. ("we"). This policy explains what information is processed when you use the app, why it is processed, and your rights. Personal data is processed under Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK) and, where applicable, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Using the app without an account

You can use the app without creating an account (as a guest). In that case your progress, name, age and settings are stored only on your device and are not sent to us.

Information we collect if you create an account

  • Account information: your email address, user ID and the sign-in method you chose (Apple, Google or email). With Sign in with Apple you can choose to hide your email.
  • Profile information: the name you optionally enter, a profile photo URL (if provided by Google/Apple), your chosen dialect and your daily goal.
  • Learning data: your lesson progress and word review statistics.

Information that stays on your device

  • Your age: stored only on your device, as a birth year, to choose the interface (child / adult).
  • Your voice recordings: audio you record with the microphone in pronunciation practice is processed only on your device; the comparison runs on the device and recordings are never uploaded. Microphone permission is requested only for this exercise.
  • Theme and other settings.

Purchases

The Nart Premium subscription is purchased through the App Store or Google Play. Your payment details stay with these stores and are not shared with us. The app only checks with the store whether your subscription is active.

Why we use information

  • To create your account, let you sign in and keep your progress,
  • To adapt lessons to your level and age,
  • To respond to support requests.

We do not show ads, we do not sell your data, and we do not track you across apps or websites.

Service providers

Account and learning data are stored on Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage). Firebase may process data on servers outside Turkey. If you sign in with Apple or Google, that company processes information for authentication under its own privacy policy.

Children

The app is designed so that children can learn Circassian too. Users under 16 see a simplified child interface, and links that leave the app are protected by a parental gate. Children under 13 need a parent's or guardian's consent to create an account; children can also use the app without an account. If you believe a child created an account without consent, contact us and we will delete it.

Retention and deletion

Account data is kept as long as your account is open. You can delete your account at any time in the app under Settings > Delete account; this permanently deletes your account and all of your data on our servers. If you cannot access the app, you can request deletion by writing to support@w8digital.com.

Your rights

Under Article 11 of KVKK and the GDPR you have the right to access your data, request correction or deletion, object to processing, and data portability. Send requests to support@w8digital.com.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit, and access is restricted by rules that let only the account owner reach their own data.

Changes

If we update this policy we will announce it in the app. For significant changes we may ask for your consent.
